How verification works

Three ways to confirm a certificate

Scan the QR code

Every VNIAS certificate carries a QR code that resolves directly to its verification page on this domain. If the code leads anywhere else, the certificate is not genuine.

Enter the ID

The identifier printed on the certificate is unique, permanent and never reissued. Search it above to open the same record the QR code points to.

Compare the record

The verification page states the recipient, the programme, the dates and the issuing body. A certificate that does not match its record is not valid.
