Verification policy

About this archive

What a VNIAS certificate record proves, what it does not, and how to raise a dispute.

What this archive is

Every certificate awarded by VitaNova International Alliance for Sciences is recorded here at the moment it is issued. Each record has a permanent address on this domain and a unique identifier that is never reused, even if the certificate is later withdrawn.

What verification proves

A record on this site confirms that VNIAS issued a certificate with that identifier, to that named recipient, for that programme, on that date. The page is generated from the same source of record that produced the certificate itself.

Verification does not authenticate a physical or digital copy of the certificate image. Images can be edited. The record on this page is authoritative; if a certificate you have been shown differs from its record here in any detail — name, programme, date — treat the certificate as invalid and contact us.

Certificate identifiers

Identifiers take the form VNIAS-WS-26-7F3KQ2M9XB:

  • VNIAS — the issuing body.
  • Two-letter programme code — EV events, SE sessions, WS workshops, CO courses.
  • Two-digit year — the year of issue.
  • Unique code — randomly generated, never sequential, so no identifier reveals anything about any other certificate.

The character set deliberately excludes I, L, O and U so that identifiers can be read aloud and typed without ambiguity. If you read one of those letters, it is a 1 or a 0.

Suspended certificates

VNIAS may suspend a certificate — for example when it was issued in error, or when the conditions of the award were not met. A suspended certificate's page remains online permanently and states its suspension clearly. Its identifier is never released or reassigned.

A verification link that once worked will always resolve. If an identifier returns no record at all, no such certificate was ever issued.

Privacy

These records publish only what already appears on the face of the certificate: the recipient's name, the programme, and the dates. Email addresses and all other personal details are held privately by VNIAS and are never published here.

Reporting a problem

To report a suspected forgery, a factual error in a record, or to request removal of your name, write to vitanova.science@gmail.com. Include the certificate identifier where possible. Enquiries are answered within two working days.
